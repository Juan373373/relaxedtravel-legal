Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

1. Controller and contact

Relaxed Travel Europe is operated by Relaxed Travel, residing in Spain.

Data protection and support contact: relaxedtravel.support@gmail.com.

2. What this app does

Relaxed Travel Europe provides general travel information for European destinations, including practical checklists, connectivity guidance, transport basics, safety notes, attraction reminders, and offline travel packs.

The app is not a substitute for official government, embassy, consulate, carrier, transport authority, attraction, insurance, or medical advice.

3. Information we do not intentionally collect

We do not intentionally collect:

  • passport numbers
  • national ID numbers
  • payment card numbers
  • sensitive health data
  • biometric data
  • precise location data without explicit consent, if such a feature is added later

4. Information we may collect

4.1 App usage data

We may collect anonymized or pseudonymized app interaction data to understand which features are useful, such as country views, checklist completions, search queries, offline pack downloads, and feedback submissions.

4.2 Device information

We may collect basic device information such as app version, device model, operating system version, locale, and crash diagnostics.

4.3 Feedback

If you submit feedback, we may store the content of that feedback, the selected country/topic, app version, and device information needed to investigate reports of outdated or incorrect information.

Please do not include passport numbers, ID numbers, payment card details, health data, or other sensitive personal information in feedback.

4.4 Advertising data

The free version of the app may contain advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Our advertising partners may process certain device and app interaction data for advertising measurement, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and, where permitted and consented, personalized ads.

For users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and other regions where required, we use a consent management process, such as the Google User Messaging Platform, before showing personalized advertising.

4.5 Affiliate links

Some links to eSIM providers, insurance providers, transport services, attraction tickets, or other travel services may be partner links. If you use them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

We may record anonymized click events to measure partner performance. We do not collect your payment card data. Any purchase you make with a third-party provider is subject to that provider’s privacy policy and terms.

4.6 Backend and storage

We may use backend services, such as Supabase or similar hosting/database providers, to store travel content, feedback, offline pack manifests, affiliate click logs, and app configuration data.

4.7 Future premium purchases

If we later offer premium features inside the Android app, purchases may be processed through Google Play Billing. Google Play may collect payment information directly. We do not collect your payment card number. We may store purchase entitlement information needed to unlock features.

5. Legal bases for processing

Where the GDPR applies, we may rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent for analytics, personalized advertising, and marketing-related processing where required.
  • Legitimate interests for app security, crash diagnosis, abuse prevention, content improvement, and service optimization.
  • Contract where processing is necessary to provide a requested feature, such as storing feedback you submit or enabling a premium purchase you request.
  • Legal obligation where we must retain or process data to comply with applicable law.

6. Third-party services

We may use the following categories of service providers:

  • Google Play for app distribution and, if enabled, in-app billing

We retain feedback, diagnostics, and operational data only as long as needed to investigate issues, improve content accuracy, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.
